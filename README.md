# Hi, I'm Senerity 👋

I work at the intersection of **Robotics, AI, and Autonomous Systems** — building machines that perceive, decide, and act on their own.

## 🔬 Research & Work

- 🚗 **Autonomous Driving** — Autoware / Apollo, from perception to planning
- 🤖 **Robotics** — ROS 2 · Nav2 · SLAM & state estimation
- 🎮 **Control** — PID · MPC · LQR · Reinforcement Learning
- 🧠 **Perception & Deep Learning** — LiDAR & camera perception (PointNet, CenterPoint, ...), 3D detection
- 🦾 **Embodied AI** — VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models, end-to-end robot policies
- 🚁 **UAV Systems** — PX4-based flight stacks, offboard control, and aerial autonomy
